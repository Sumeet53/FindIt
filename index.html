Tips for using FindIt

📍
Be specific with spots: Write "Top shelf of pantry, in blue bin" rather than just "Kitchen". You'll find it 10x faster.
🤝
Never lose lent tools: When a friend borrows your drill or ladder, tap "Lend" so you always remember who has it.
🔒
Private & Offline: All your belongings are saved directly to this device in your browser. Nothing is sent to external servers.
