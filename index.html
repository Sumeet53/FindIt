Why do we have a streak? Here is the cognitive science behind FindIt.

The 14-Day Physical Memory Retention Curve

Human brains naturally discard spatial location memory within 14 to 21 days when an item isn’t actively seen or used. When an item sits tucked in a closet for months, your cognitive map erases its exact shelf coordinates.

Opening FindIt for just 10 seconds a day stimulates synaptic recall, keeping your entire home inventory fresh in active memory. You never feel that sudden sinking anxiety of lost passports, missing keys, or forgotten tools.

FindIt User Manual

How to Use FindIt & Master Physical Memory

Simple habits that ensure you never lose anything in your house again.

⚡ The Golden Rule: Log as you put away

Log the exact second your hand leaves the object. Tap “I Just Put Something Away” and speak. Hands are full? Just speak into your phone!

📸 Photo Memory Clues

People recall visual landmarks faster than text labels. Snap a photo of the drawer or box so you recognise it immediately 6 months later.

🚶 Bulk Tour Mode (Fast Setup)

Never add 300 items one-by-one. Walk into a room, open a drawer, and speak: “Hammer, pliers, drill, tape measure” to batch-add a whole shelf in 20 seconds.

🤝 Never Lose Lent Tools

When a neighbor borrows a ladder or tool, tap Lend on its card. You will always know who borrowed what and when it is expected back.
