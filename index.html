The FindIt Philosophy

A search engine for physical atoms, not digital pixels.

Traditional home inventories fail because maintaining them feels like a job. FindIt is built on three strict principles:

1. Spatial Hierarchy & Visual Anchors: Items are mapped via Room › Zone › Container › Landmark. You don't just know which room it's in; you know which box and what it's behind.
2. Accountability & Verification Freshness: Memory drifts. FindIt rates confidence based on when you last physically laid eyes on the item, prompting rapid 30-second spot-checks.
3. Zero-Build Vanilla PWA: Works 100% offline, stores data safely on your device, and can be hosted effortlessly anywhere without compilation steps.

Custodian Responsibility Notice

FindIt operates strictly on self-reported physical truth. The app cannot physically search your drawers or track GPS microchips.

  • When you borrow or lend an item, update the ledger immediately.
  • When you move an item, update its location tag.
  • Run periodic spot-checks to keep verification fresh.

Your data is stored locally in your browser's LocalStorage and will never leave your device unless you export a backup.
